分组
group by(x)靠x分组     with rollup最后计算总数(不能同时使用order by一起用)

方式1：推荐，执行效率高于方式2.
SELECT department_id,MAX(salary) 
FROM employees WHERE department_id IN (10,20,30,40)
GROUP BY department_id 
HAVING MAX(salary) > 10000; 

方式2： SELECT department_id,MAX(salary)
FROM employees GROUP BY department_id
HAVING MAX(salary) > 10000 
AND department_id IN (10,20,30,40);

要求:1.出现聚合函数要用having代替where
     2.having不能单独出现,必须和group by()一起出现
